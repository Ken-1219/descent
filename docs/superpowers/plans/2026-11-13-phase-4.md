# Phase 4: Tiered obstacles and enemies (implementation plan)

> For agentic workers: execute task by task. The spec is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-phase-4-design.md`.

**Goal:** Every §8 family at T1–T4, the 3 Trapsetter traps and the 3 items. All of it is data-driven, has a tell in the state, and is shown as 34 scenes. Each scene serves as a test, a Lab loop and a playable URL.

**Architecture:**
- Tier tables live in `content.ts`.
- Rules live in one `step()` in `sim.ts`, in the fixed order from §4.
- `tells(state)` exposes each danger one turn ahead.
- Scenes are data in `src/shared/scenes.ts`.
- The two renderers (the Phaser scene and the Lab canvas) share `look()` and `choreograph()` from `sprites.ts`.

**Tech stack:** TypeScript 6, Phaser 4.2.1, Vitest 5, bun.

## Global constraints

- Use integers, arrays and seeded randomness only in `src/shared`. No `Math.random`, `Date` or floats.
- Every tuning number lives in `content.ts`.
- Turn order: player, then mechanisms, then enemies by id, then torch, then damage, then end check.
- The animation lock is 120 ms or less per turn.
- No new runtime dependencies. Raw hex appears only in `content.ts` and `style.css`.
- Scenes must not reach the production bundle.

## Execution

Inline, in this session. Every task edits `sim.ts`, `content.ts` or `scenes.ts`, so parallel subagents would conflict on the same files. A subagent runs the independent review at the end.

### Task 1: rng step and tier tables
- `rng.ts`: add `rand(a): [value, next]`, and rebuild `mulberry32` on top of it.
- `content.ts`: add `Family`, `Tier`, the tables `SPIKES`, `GOBLINS`, `IMPS`, `MECHANISMS`, `SKELETONS`, `PITS`, `DARK`, `TRAPS`, `ITEMS`, and the constants `PIT_DROP` and `PACK_RADIUS`.
- Test: a frame-name existence test, and `cost` that follows §8 (1, 2, 3, 5, +1 for enemies).

### Task 2: sim rules
`sim.ts` changes:
- New types and fields from the spec.
- `start()`: packs, buried spikes and tiers.
- `step()` in the spec's order.
- New exports: `tells()`, `spikeStage()`, `bfs()`.
- Phase 3 tests updated where A2 changes the timing (the goblin's wind-up), and hurt events gain `at`.

### Task 3: scenes and their tests
- `scenes.ts`: 34 scenes.
- `tests/shared.test.ts`, one test per scene:
  - the exact `hurt` list
  - the `at` checks
  - a tell exists at the hit tile with the same cause one turn before every hurt
- Item tests (swap, use costs a turn, no item = no turn, potion cap, oil cap, bomb with skeletons).
- A determinism test: every scene replayed 100 times gives the same JSON.

### Task 4: art and look
- `sprites.ts` PACK additions: `spikes-0` to `spikes-2`, `hole`, `plate-blue`, `plate-blue-down`, `plate-red`, `plate-red-down`.
- `art.ts` sprites: `slit`, `missing-step`, `crack`, `mortar`, `bars`, `chain`, `chain-crack`, `crumble`, `den`, `brazier`, `brazier-lit`, `mark-aim`, `mark-line`, `trap-mark`, `digit-1` to `digit-4`.
- `look(state)` and `choreograph(events, next)`.
- New ANIMS entries with a hold frame.

### Task 5: game scene and HUD
- `scene.ts`: props re-sync from tiles every turn; enemy kinds; tells drawn from `look()`; motions from `choreograph()`.
- `ui.ts` and `game.ts`: item slot icon.
- `?scene=<id>` loads a scene in dev; `load()` in the dev hook accepts a scene id.

### Task 6: Lab Bestiary tab
- Grid of families × tiers, plus a strip for traps and items.
- Each cell is a canvas loop with pause, step and 0.25×, plus its caption and cost.
- A filmstrip mode per cell, for the §15.4 review.
