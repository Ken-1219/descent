# Phase 11i Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (inline here). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** A rollover ladder that keeps the players' choices (A121–A122), "Your mark" on the intro (A123), a sixth crowd playtest, and then the release, which stops for the subreddit name.

**Architecture:**
- The ladder lives in `src/shared/crowd.ts`. The trap slot is one line in `gen.ts`.
- The facts come from `yoursFor()` in `src/server/game.ts` and travel in the floor response. The local harness mirrors them.
- The intro section is in `game.ts`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest with the Devvit test harness, bun.

## Global Constraints
- Rules only in `src/shared`; no floats, `Date` or `Math.random` there.
- Tuning goes in `content.ts`: `LADDER_REROLLS`, `TRAP_MOVES`, and a cap on how many floors one rollover may try.
- Targets on at least 448 crowd rollovers: a survivor's trap set aside in under 5%, the winning curse in under 1%. The rollover stays inside 10 s.
- The intro fits at 360×640 with no inner scroll.
